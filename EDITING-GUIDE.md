ELICIT GREEN - READY TO PREVIEW

1. Unzip this package.
2. Open the extracted folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open the report in your browser. No server, Git or installation needed.
4. Use the sidebar to navigate. Appendices and References are linked.
5. When happy, copy index.html, appendices.html, references.html and the assets folder into your existing repository, preserving your other project files.
6. Do not commit .env or .venv.
